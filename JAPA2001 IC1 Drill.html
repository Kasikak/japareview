How answers are checked

Romaji turns into hiragana as you type (shown under the box). Both Hepburn and kunrei spellings work: shi/si, chi/ti, tsu/tu, fu/hu, ji/zi, sha/sya. Double a consonant for small っ (itta → いった). Type nn or n' for ん when it comes before a vowel. Long vowels: ou or ō.

Kanji answers are accepted where the kanji is in Units 12–13; the hiragana is always accepted. For particles, wa counts for は and o for を.

If your answer differs only by a small っ, a long vowel, small ゃゅょ or ゛゜, you'll see an orange Almost. It still counts as wrong, because it would on paper.
